Frequently Asked Questions

Does Edge Voice work without internet?

Yes! Edge Voice processes commands locally. Internet is only needed for initial setup and firmware updates.

What devices are compatible?

Edge Voice works with 5000+ devices including Philips Hue, Nest, Ring, Sonos, and major smart home brands.

Is my voice data private?

Absolutely. All voice processing happens on the device. We never store or transmit your voice recordings.

What's the warranty?

Every Edge Voice comes with a 2-year warranty and 30-day money-back guarantee.
